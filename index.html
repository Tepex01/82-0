How to play. Each spin lands on an NBA team, and its roster is hidden. Type a player's name, or a position (PG, SG, SF, PF, C) to see who plays there. Starters have to match their position; your sixth man can play anything. You get two rerolls. Daily mode gives everyone the same teams, and once you finish you can open the answer key.

How scoring works. Every player has an OVR from 40 to 99 based on fantasy points per game (ESPN standard scoring) over the last three seasons, with the most recent season counting most. Rookies are rated by draft slot. Your Team OVR is a weighted average: each starter counts 17.5% and the sixth man 12.5%. Your record comes straight from Team OVR, so the same lineup always gets the same result: 87.5 or higher goes 82–0, 86 is about 71 wins, 82 is about 58.

Rosters from ESPN, stats from Basketball-Reference. Unofficial fan game, not affiliated with the NBA.
